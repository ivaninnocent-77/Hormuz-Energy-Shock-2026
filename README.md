# The Strait of Hormuz Energy Shock, 2026

This project is an end-to-end data analytics portfolio developed using **R and R Markdown** to investigate the economic and global trade consequences of the 2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis. It brings together ten datasets covering daily and historical crude oil prices, petrol prices in 14 countries, economic impacts across 17 countries, geopolitical events, and shipping flows involving crude oil, liquefied natural gas (LNG), fertiliser and agricultural products. The central objective is to understand how disruption to a major global energy route affects oil markets, fuel costs, national economies and international supply chains.

The analysis addresses **32 business and policy questions** using data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistical hypothesis testing, time-series analysis, regression, correlation, bootstrapping, hierarchical clustering and principal component analysis. It uses R packages including `tidyverse`, `ggplot2`, `broom` and `knitr` to produce statistical summaries, visualisations and a reproducible analytical report. The project demonstrates practical applications of R in economic analysis, global trade monitoring, risk assessment and evidence-based decision-making.

Repository contents: **Portfolio.Rmd** contains the main analytical report and R code; **data/** stores the ten CSV datasets used in the analysis; **R/helpers.R** contains reusable data-cleaning functions, formatting utilities and visualisation settings; **install_packages.R** provides the package installation setup; and **app/** contains the companion interactive Shiny dashboard for exploring oil prices, historical shocks, petrol prices, country risks and trade flows. The report can be rendered as a PDF using R Markdown, while the dashboard can be launched in R with shiny::runApp("app"). Together, these files provide a reproducible project demonstrating practical applications of R in statistical analysis, economic risk assessment, data visualisation, global trade monitoring and evidence-based decision-making.
